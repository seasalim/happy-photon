NEW IN HAPPY PHOTON 0.2.4

Move faster. See truer. Keep every decision.

Leave Lightroom, keep the work

Import ratings, flags, color labels, and supported crops from Lightroom Classic. Opt-in XMP sidecars keep those decisions portable without ever rewriting the original photograph.

Every edit has a way back

A persistent Develop history records adjustments, rotation, horizon, and crops. Jump to any step, undo and redo, preview it in the Navigator, or clear the path above it.

Cull at full size

Open the new Browse Loupe with E, Enter, or Space. Navigate, assess, zoom to 1:1, and move directly into Compare or Develop without giving up your place or selection.

Color that reaches the screen intact

Happy Photon now follows the active Windows monitor profile and carries correct sRGB intent into the macOS compositor, so the preview you judge stays true to the pipeline.

Also new: cleaner wavelet chroma denoising, more responsive capture sharpening at Fit, Lightroom-familiar shortcuts, denser thumbnail rows, quieter monochrome controls, overlay scrollbars, and Fit views that never enlarge beyond 1:1.
