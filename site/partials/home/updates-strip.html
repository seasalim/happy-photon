NEW IN HAPPY PHOTON 0.2.5

Shape the light. Right where it matters.

Guide the eye with local masks

Draw a linear gradient across a sky or place a radial mask around a subject. Feather the edges for a smooth transition, and choose the inside or outside of a radial mask.

Adjust local light and color

Change Exposure, Temperature, Tint, and Saturation within each mask. Brighten a face, cool a background, or soften a patch of strong color without changing the whole frame.

Place each adjustment precisely

Drag mask controls on the photograph, enter numeric geometry, or start at the center of the view. Crop and Locals tools sit beneath the histogram.

Another way to download for Windows

The Microsoft Store remains recommended. A Windows x64 ZIP on GitHub Releases provides a direct-download fallback with manual updates.

Local adjustments stay non-destructive, work with undo and redo, and carry through to your exported photographs.
