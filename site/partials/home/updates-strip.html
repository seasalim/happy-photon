NEW IN HAPPY PHOTON 0.2.1

Judge more clearly. Deliver more precisely.

An Export workspace

Export is a full workspace beside Browse and Develop: a filmstrip of the selected captures, a preview that proofs the output pixels, and a queue that keeps running while you move on.

RAW + JPEG as one capture

Turn on J+R to fold a same-name pair into one tile that is flagged, rated, and labeled together. In Develop, J|R switches between the camera JPEG and the RAW without losing your place.

Versions, compare, and before/after

Keep up to eight interpretations of one file. Compare two to four photos side by side in Browse, split before and after in Develop, and press and hold for an instant 1:1 loupe—all synchronized.

Lens corrections from a real database

RAW files matched in the bundled Lensfun database receive distortion, chromatic aberration, and vignetting corrections alongside embedded prescriptions. Nikon lens identities are recovered from maker notes.

Also new: vertical and horizontal perspective with an alignment grid, wavelet luminance noise reduction, right-click copy path, reveal, and delete in Browse, a Linux AppImage, and a visible control for every keyboard shortcut.
