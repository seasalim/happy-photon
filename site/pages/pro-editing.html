COLOR SCIENCE UNDER THE HOOD

Built to hold up when you push the photograph.

Happy Photon keeps image formation, creative edits, and output conversion in a deliberate order. Preview and export share that path, so the finished file follows the photograph you developed.

  1. 01Characterize

    Measured as-shot white balance, built-in camera characterization, and DCP profiles when available.

  2. 02Form the image

    Highlight reconstruction and a scene-referred AgX tone engine shape RAW data without a baked-in picture style.

  3. 03Shape color

    White balance, color mixer, saturation, and skin-aware vibrance operate through a wide-gamut perceptual color stage.

  4. 04Finish detail

    Capture sharpening, noise reduction, output sharpening, vignette, and deterministic grain resolve at the right stage.

  5. 05Deliver

    The result converts to an embedded sRGB or Display P3 profile and carries normalized metadata under your control.

DEEP WHERE IT COUNTS

The controls to finish a photograph. Not a cockpit to manage.

LIGHT / TONE

Recover the scene, then shape it

Set exposure, highlights, shadows, contrast, and RAW highlight recovery. Refine the result with a composite curve or independent red, green, and blue curves.

COLOR

Correct neutrals. Direct attention.

Use Kelvin and tint, Auto white balance, or an eyedropper. Then work with saturation, skin-aware vibrance, and an eight-band hue, saturation, and lightness mixer.

DETAIL

Separate capture from delivery

Apply source-aware capture sharpening, chroma noise reduction, and RAW noise reduction. Choose independent Screen or Print sharpening when you export.

FINISH

Make the frame feel complete

Crop, rotate, and straighten the horizon. Add a controlled vignette or deterministic monochrome grain that stays stable from preview to finished output.

MEASURE WHAT YOU SEE

Scopes for decisions, not decoration.

Read the display histogram, luminance waveform, or RAW sensor histogram. Toggle highlight and shadow clipping, inspect before and after, zoom in device pixels, or enter Assess mode for an invariant mid-gray surround and white reference.

  • Display histogram
  • Luminance waveform
  • RAW sensor histogram
  • Clipping overlays

RAW IS A FIRST-CLASS SOURCE

Camera data gets a photographic path.

Happy Photon starts from the sensor and builds the image deliberately: measured as-shot white balance, highlight reconstruction, camera characterization, optional DCP profiles, and true monochrome RAW handling.

  • Scene-referred renderingAgX maps RAW scene values into a display-ready photograph.
  • Camera profilesUse discovered DCP profiles when a compatible profile is available.
  • Embedded lens dataApply qualified DNG and Fujifilm RAF lens corrections where supported.
  • True monochromeKeep monochrome sensor data neutral through preview and export.

A PROFESSIONAL HANDOFF

Export the file the job needs. Keep the original safe.

Deliver JPEG, PNG, WebP, or lossless 16-bit TIFF in sRGB or Display P3. Preserve normalized EXIF, strip GPS when needed, and rely on collision protection that refuses to overwrite a loaded original.

JPEG · PNG · WebPEveryday delivery formats with explicit sizing and color space.

FOCUSED BY DESIGN

Pro processing does not have to mean feature sprawl.

Happy Photon is built around global photographic adjustments and a fast folder-to-export workflow. It does not currently include local masks, layers, HDR output, or custom output profiles.
