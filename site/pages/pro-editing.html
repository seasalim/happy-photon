COLOR SCIENCE UNDER THE HOOD

Built to hold up when you push the photograph.

Happy Photon keeps image formation, creative edits, and output conversion in a deliberate order. Preview and export share that path, so the finished file follows the photograph you developed.

  1. 01Characterize

    Measured as-shot white balance, built-in camera characterization, and DCP profiles when available.

  2. 02Form the image

    Lens corrections, highlight reconstruction, and a scene-referred AgX tone engine shape RAW data without a baked-in picture style.

  3. 03Shape color

    White balance, color mixer, saturation, and skin-aware vibrance operate through a wide-gamut perceptual color stage.

  4. 04Finish detail

    Capture sharpening, noise reduction, output sharpening, vignette, and deterministic grain resolve at the right stage.

  5. 05Deliver

    The result converts to an embedded sRGB or Display P3 profile and carries normalized metadata under your control.

DEEP WHERE IT COUNTS

The controls to finish a photograph. Not a cockpit to manage.

Work across the whole image or draw linear and radial masks for local Exposure, Temperature, Tint, and Saturation. Feather the transitions, position masks on the image or numerically, and refine each adjustment with undo and redo.

LIGHT / TONE

Recover the scene, then shape it

Set exposure, highlights, shadows, contrast, and RAW highlight recovery. Refine the result with a composite curve or independent red, green, and blue curves.

COLOR

Correct neutrals. Direct attention.

Use Kelvin and tint, Auto white balance, or an eyedropper. Then work with saturation, skin-aware vibrance, and an eight-band hue, saturation, and lightness mixer.

DETAIL

Separate capture from delivery

Apply source-aware capture sharpening that responds at Fit, wavelet luminance and chroma denoising, and RAW noise reduction. Choose independent Screen or Print sharpening when you export.

FINISH

Make the frame feel complete

Crop, rotate, straighten the horizon, and correct vertical and horizontal perspective against an alignment grid. Add a controlled vignette or deterministic monochrome grain that stays stable from preview to finished output.

MEASURE WHAT YOU SEE

Scopes for decisions, not decoration.

Read the display histogram, luminance waveform, or RAW sensor histogram. Toggle highlight and shadow clipping, inspect before and after in a synchronized split, press and hold for a 1:1 loupe, zoom in device pixels, or enter Assess mode for an invariant mid-gray surround and white reference.

Windows previews follow the active monitor profile; macOS previews carry sRGB intent into the system compositor. Fit views never enlarge a photograph past 1:1, so small images stay at their native size.

KEEP THE WORK MOVING

Carry your decisions through the whole shoot.

  • Bring your decisions with youImport ratings, flags, color labels, and supported crops from Lightroom Classic. Opt-in XMP sidecars keep those decisions portable without rewriting original pixels.
  • Cull at full sizeOpen Browse Loupe with E, Enter, or Space. Keep the folder tree and assessment controls close, then move into Compare or Develop without losing your selection.
  • Keep RAW and JPEG togetherJ+R groups a same-name pair into one capture with shared flags, ratings, and labels. J|R switches between the camera JPEG and RAW in Develop without losing your viewport.
  • Compare and explore alternativesCompare two to four photographs in sync. Keep up to eight independent versions of one file, each with its own edits and optional label, and export the interpretations side by side.
  • Explore with a visible historyPersistent Develop history records committed adjustments, rotation, horizon, and crops. Jump to a step, preview it in the Navigator, undo and redo, or clear the steps above a chosen point.
  • Stay focused on the photographsLightroom-familiar shortcuts, dense thumbnail rows, monochrome controls, and overlay scrollbars keep the workspace compact and familiar.

RAW IS A FIRST-CLASS SOURCE

Camera data gets a photographic path.

Happy Photon starts from the sensor and builds the image deliberately: measured as-shot white balance, highlight reconstruction, camera characterization, optional DCP profiles, database and embedded lens corrections, and true monochrome RAW handling.

  • Scene-referred renderingAgX maps RAW scene values into a display-ready photograph.
  • Camera profilesUse discovered DCP profiles when a compatible profile is available.
  • Lens correctionsCorrect distortion, chromatic aberration, and vignetting using embedded DNG and Fujifilm RAF prescriptions or an exact camera and lens match from the bundled Lensfun database. Nikon lens identities are recovered from maker notes.
  • True monochromeKeep monochrome sensor data neutral through preview and export.

A PROFESSIONAL HANDOFF

Export the file the job needs. Keep the original safe.

Deliver JPEG, PNG, WebP, or lossless 16-bit TIFF in sRGB or Display P3. Preserve normalized EXIF, strip GPS when needed, and rely on collision protection that refuses to overwrite a loaded original.

The dedicated Export workspace keeps selected captures in a filmstrip and proofs the output pixels before delivery. Its queue keeps running while you continue working.

FOCUSED BY DESIGN
