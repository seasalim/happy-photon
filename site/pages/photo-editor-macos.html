IS IT A FIT?

For local shoots and the decisions that finish them

Happy Photon gives Apple Silicon Mac users a focused workflow for personal RAW, JPEG, and mixed-format shoots. It works directly with folders and concentrates on selection, global photographic adjustments, presets, and export.

  • No import or cloud upload is required to begin.
  • No required account, subscription, advertising, or product telemetry.
  • Edits, ratings, flags, and color labels are stored in a local catalog.
  • Exports create separate JPEG, PNG, or WebP files.

INSTALL ON MACOS

Use the signed and notarized release

    Check the Mac

    The public build requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs are not supported by the initial release.

  2. 02

    Download Happy Photon

    Double-click Happy Photon.app and choose Open at the macOS safety prompt. You can try it from Downloads or drag it to Applications to keep it with your other apps.

FILES AND CAMERAS

Bring a JPEG, RAW, or mixed camera folder

Standard image support includes JPEG (commonly shown as JPG), PNG, BMP, GIF, TIFF, and WebP. RAW support is verified for CR2/CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, RAF, and RW2. NRW, ORF, and PEF open through the same decoder but are not part of the verified set.

Verify the camera, not only the extension.

RAW decoding uses the bundled, audited LibRaw 0.22.2 generation. Support varies by camera model and capture mode, especially for newer bodies. HEIC/HEIF support can also vary with the available codec. Nikon high-efficiency (HE and HE*) NEF files are not supported and are reported as unsupported rather than opened.

ORIGINAL-FILE SAFETY

Your source photographs remain unchanged

Happy Photon saves edit instructions, flags, ratings, color labels, and presets in ~/Pictures/Happy Photon Catalog/ by default. Regenerable previews and thumbnails use ~/Library/Caches/Happy Photon, and Settings can stage a catalog move. Original image files remain where they started.

Happy Photon is pre-1.0 software. Keep normal backups of important photographs and verify the camera formats you depend on.

CURRENT SCOPE

A smaller workflow by design

The current release does not support Intel Macs, local masks, layered compositing, HDR output, custom output color profiles, cloud/mobile synchronization, or Lightroom-edit migration.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you download

What happens the first time I open it?

macOS shows its standard safety confirmation for an app downloaded from the internet. Choose Open to continue. Public Happy Photon releases are Developer ID signed and notarized.

Does Happy Photon support Intel Macs?

No. The initial public macOS target is Apple Silicon running macOS 14 or later.

Do my photographs need to be copied into the app?

No. Choose an existing folder and browse it in place. Happy Photon keeps its catalog data separately and leaves the original image files untouched.
